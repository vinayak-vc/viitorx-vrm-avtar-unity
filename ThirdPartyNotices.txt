License: MIT License
Copyright (c) 2021 geaxgx
Upstream: https://github.com/geaxgx/depthai_blazepose

A copy of this project is included at python-sidecar~/depthai_blazepose/, with local modifications
for the OAK-D-PRO-W's OV9782 colour sensor. The upstream licence text ships alongside it at
python-sidecar~/depthai_blazepose/LICENSE.txt and must not be removed. It is the superseded Phase-1

13. MediaPipe palm detection and hand landmark models (Google LLC) -- MODEL WEIGHTS, NOT BUNDLED
License: Apache License, Version 2.0
Copyright (c) 2023 The MediaPipe Authors / Google LLC.
Upstream: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
Converted to ONNX and published by the OpenCV Zoo (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo

The hands-only pipeline (H-01) runs palm_detection_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx (3.9 MB) and
handpose_estimation_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx (4.1 MB). They are NOT committed (*.onnx is ignored by version
control); python-sidecar~/tools/model/fetch_hand_models.py downloads them from the OpenCV organisation's
Hugging Face mirror and rejects any file whose size and SHA-256 do not match.

-----------------------------------------------------
14. MediaPipe face detector, face mesh and face blendshape models (Google LLC) -- MODEL WEIGHTS, NOT BUNDLED
License: Apache License, Version 2.0
Copyright (c) 2023 The MediaPipe Authors / Google LLC.
Upstream: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe (the TFLite models inside face_landmarker.task)
ONNX conversions by a third party (fernandotonon, QtMeshEditor-models on Hugging Face, mocap/face/, whose NOTICE.txt
states Apache-2.0): face_detector.onnx (0.4 MB), face_landmarks.onnx (4.9 MB), face_blendshapes.onnx (1.1 MB).

The face-only pipeline (H-02) runs these. They are NOT committed (*.onnx is ignored by version control);
python-sidecar~/tools/model/fetch_face_models.py downloads them and rejects any file whose size and SHA-256 do not
match. They are a community conversion of Google's models, not an official Google release; the converter reports its
own numerical parity against Python mediapipe, which was not reproduced here.

-----------------------------------------------------
15. MediaPipe selfie segmentation models (Google LLC) -- MODEL WEIGHTS, NOT BUNDLED
License: Apache License, Version 2.0
Copyright (c) 2023 The MediaPipe Authors / Google LLC.
Upstream: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe (the TFLite selfie segmenter models)
ONNX conversions by a third party (the onnx-community organisation on Hugging Face, repositories
mediapipe_selfie_segmentation and mediapipe_selfie_segmentation_landscape, licence tag Apache-2.0):
selfie_segmenter_general.onnx (0.46 MB, 256x256) and selfie_segmenter_landscape.onnx (0.46 MB, 256x144).

The cutout pipeline (S-01, the person cut out of a camera picture) runs these. They are NOT committed
(*.onnx is ignored by version control); python-sidecar~/tools/model/fetch_cutout_models.py downloads exactly
these two files and rejects any whose size and SHA-256 do not match. They are a community conversion of
Google's models, not an official Google release; numerical parity with the TFLite originals was not
reproduced here.
