P1 **improves the signal**; P0 **remains the safety mechanism**. A `LOST` joint is not zero-filled —
its emit confidence is zeroed so `build_body_landmarks` drops it, which is byte-identical to a real
occlusion and hands the decision to the Unity LimbGate exactly as before.

## 3. Joint state model

`position · filteredPosition · velocity · acceleration · confidence · lastValidPosition ·
lastValidVelocity · lastMeasurementTimestamp · lastValidTimestamp · invalidFrameCount ·
predictedFrameCount · trackingState` — plus adaptive residual scale, running segment-length median,
frozen-run counter, and reacquisition progress. `__slots__`, scalars only, **no allocation in `update()`**.

## 4. State machine

```
(cold)──meas──►TRACKED ──low conf / suspicion──► WEAK ──no or bad meas──► PREDICTED ──window exhausted──► LOST
   ▲                                                                          │                        │
   └────────────────── blended reacquisition (never a teleport) ◄──────────────┴────────────────────────┘
```

`WEAK` still **uses** the measurement at reduced weight — it is a down-weight, not a discard.

## 5. Velocity / prediction

Velocity from real timestamps (`Δp/Δt`, `dt` clamped against zero/negative/huge stamps), lightly
smoothed (β 0.5 — deliberately *not* a second large-latency filter). Acceleration is a validation
signal only. Prediction is `lastValid + v·dt + ½·a·dt²` with a bounded acceleration term, capped by
**max 6 frames**, **max 0.30 m total displacement**, and per-frame velocity decay 0.80 so a long gap
coasts to a stop rather than flying off.

## 6. Plausibility checks

Seven signals combine into a 0…1 suspicion score (≥1.0 = reject, 0<s<1 = WEAK):

1. **confidence** below the emit threshold
2. **residual** vs the kinematic prediction, against an *adaptive* per-joint scale (a dancing wrist earns
   a wider gate than a still hip; fixed metre thresholds would either clip dancing or pass spikes)
3. **speed** > 8 m/s → hard reject
4. **acceleration** > 120 m/s²
5. **depth consistency** — uses the existing 5×5/p30 result unchanged: depth unmeasured, or measured Z
   contradicting the prediction by > 0.20 m
6. **FROZEN** — joint pinned within 4 mm for ≥12 frames *while its parent moved ≥5 cm*. **This is the
   check that catches the actual observed failure**: a frozen joint has near-zero residual, speed and
   acceleration, so nothing else can see it.
7. **segment length** vs the joint's own running median (shoulder→elbow→wrist, hip→knee→ankle)

**Causal-only by design.** The brief's N-2…N+2 comparison needs lookahead, and lookahead is latency.
Instead a suspicious sample is down-weighted, and if consecutive suspicious samples *agree with each
other* the tracker promotes back to TRACKED within 1–2 frames — so `A→B→C→D→E` (real fast motion)
survives while `A→B→X→B` (spike) is damped, at zero added latency.

## 7. Depth consistency

The existing OAK-D depth path is **unchanged**. The tracker consumes `measured[]` and the back-projected
Z purely as a validation signal.

## 8. Reacquisition

Never a teleport. On return from PREDICTED/LOST the tracker blends `predicted → measurement` over 5
frames (1/N, 2/N …). Critically, the returning sample is **not** judged by the tight residual gate
(which is tuned for continuous tracking) — only sanity-checked against `max_speed × elapsed gap`.
Logged: `reacquisitionStart`, duration, `initialError`.

## 9. Unit tests — **37 / 37 assertions pass**

All 15 brief cases plus neighbour-constraint and a timing smoke test:
stationary · constant velocity · fast motion · isolated spike · **high-confidence spike** · 3/5/10-frame
dropout · prediction · prediction expiry · reacquisition · large-error reacquisition · irregular
timestamps · zero/invalid measurement · depth inconsistency.

### Two real bugs the tests caught

1. **Reacquisition teleported.** Test 11 showed `moved 0.0000 of 0.1500 m` and `reacquire_count == 0`,
   yet still converged — recovery was going through the *coherence* path (a hard accept), because the
   continuous-tracking residual gate rejected every returning sample. Fixed with a dedicated gap-recovery
   path. This is precisely what the brief forbids and it would not have been visible from the aggregate
   statistics.
2. **Coherence promotion zeroed velocity**, stalling a joint mid-swing. Now re-seeds velocity from the
   agreeing samples.

## 11. Adversarial — **6 / 6 detected**

Faults injected into the real dancing trajectory; error measured against the *un-injected* real
trajectory as ground truth.

| case | detected | state | peak err | residual err |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A hi-conf 0.8 m wrist error (1 frame) | **YES** | PREDICTED | 0.0020 m | 0.0110 m |
| B **hi-conf FROZEN wrist, 20 frames** | **YES** | TRACKED,WEAK | 0.0810 m | **0.0000 m** |
| C 5-frame wrist disappearance | **YES** | PREDICTED | 0.0486 m | 0.0458 m |
| D 10-frame knee disappearance | **YES** | LOST,PRED | 0.0915 m | 0.0458 m |
| E hi-conf knee teleport (6 frames) | **YES** | LOST,PRED | 0.5629 m | 0.6682 m |
| F 1-frame ankle spike | **YES** | PREDICTED | 0.0029 m | 0.0219 m |

Case **A** — an 0.8 m error at confidence 0.95 — leaves a **2 mm** trace. Case **B** is the exact
real-world failure and recovers with **zero** residual error.

**Case E is the weak result** and is reported as such: the tracker correctly *refuses* the teleport,
exhausts its 6-frame prediction window and goes LOST — at which point the joint is marked invalid and
the P0 LimbGate holds the last good rotation (the layered design working). The 0.67 m residual is the
cost of having no ground truth for 6 frames while the subject is dancing fast. Bridging that needs
longer-horizon prediction and blended recovery — **P1-3 / P1-5**, explicitly out of P1-1 scope.

## 12. False positive / false rejection analysis

Initial implementation rejected **4.6–13.5%** of joint-frames on legitimate motion (38% not-TRACKED
during fast arms). Root cause, measured not guessed: the **segment-length check fired 3170 times** and
was acting as a *veto*. The brief says an inconsistent neighbour should "reduce confidence" — so its
contribution is now capped at 0.5 (it can never single-handedly reject), tolerance widened to 40%, and
the median keeps learning during WEAK.

Result: **PREDICTED 13.5% → 0.3%, LOST 0.0%**, with medians unchanged.

A second regression — P1's *max* displacement exceeding P0's — was traced to WEAK catch-up: under-
following a questionable sample builds an offset that was repaid in one frame. Bounded by an output
step limiter (≤1.2 × the measurement's own step). The first attempt did not bind because `meas_step`
was computed from `lastValid`, which during WEAK holds the blended output — inflating the budget by
exactly the offset it was meant to bound. Fixed to measurement-to-measurement: R-wrist **+40.5% → +6.1%**.

*(An earlier in-pipeline measurement read 0.000/1.010 ms — that was `time.time()`'s ~1 ms Windows
resolution quantising a sub-millisecond signal, not real data. Switched to `perf_counter`.)*

### Latency investigation (the P0 §4d Finding B follow-up)

Per-stage, real OAK-D, no subject: camera→host 14–72 ms (highly variable — **queue backlog**, not
compute), host→pose **21.4 ms** (RTMW3D inference — the dominant cost), pose→depth 9.1 ms, tracker
0.085 ms, host→UDP 31.2 ms.

**The 131 ms camera→host figure measured with a subject is queue wait, not processing.** Inference at
~21 ms plus decode cannot sustain the 30 fps sensor (measured 21.3 fps with a person), so the
`maxSize=4` RGB queue fills and drains oldest-first. **P1-1 is not the cause and does not worsen it**;
the fix is queue policy or inference cost, neither of which is in P1-1 scope.

## 15. Remaining problems

1. **Case E** — a sustained high-confidence teleport longer than the prediction window ends in LOST with
   slow recovery during fast motion. Needs P1-3/P1-5.
2. **Per-joint confidence is unavailable in the evaluation data.** `recv_log` carries per-*limb*
   confidence (min of 3 joints), so each joint was fed its limb's value. The live sidecar path does use
   true per-joint confidence; only the offline evaluation is coarser.
3. **Confidence remains un-normalised** (audit F-08). P1-1 works around it via kinematics rather than
   fixing the scale; normalisation is P2.
4. **Segment-length medians are learned online** with no anthropometric prior, so the first ~8 frames of
   a joint have no neighbour check.
5. **Evaluation replays post-P0 logged positions**, not raw pre-smoothing keypoints — the tracker sees a
   signal already capped at 0.35 m. In production it sits in the same place, so this matches, but the
   true raw spike magnitudes are larger than anything in these numbers.
